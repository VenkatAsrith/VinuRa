# Our Approach

We don't believe every client needs a complicated website.

Sometimes a business needs a simple landing page.

Sometimes it needs a complete website.

Sometimes it needs a custom system.

We start with the requirement and build around it.

### Understand

We first understand:

- What the business does
- Who the audience is
- What the client wants to achieve
- What information needs to be presented
- What action visitors should take

### Design

We create the visual direction around the business instead of forcing the business into a predefined template.

### Develop

We turn the design into a responsive and production-ready website.

### Optimize

We review:

- Mobile experience
- Performance
- Responsiveness
- Accessibility
- SEO foundations
- User experience

### Launch

Once everything is ready, we deploy the website and help the client establish their digital presence.
